3. Services Page

# Our Healthcare Services

At K J Hospital, healthcare services are focused on supporting patients through consultation, evaluation, treatment and follow-up care.

## Medical Consultation

Medical consultation is an important first step in understanding a patient's health concerns.

During consultation, healthcare professionals may evaluate symptoms, review relevant medical information and recommend appropriate investigations or treatment based on the patient's condition.

---

## Emergency & Urgent Care

Patients experiencing a medical emergency should seek immediate medical attention.

K J Hospital's public listing indicates 24-hour operation; however, patients should contact the hospital directly to confirm the availability and scope of emergency services before relying on this information.

**For a medical emergency, seek immediate professional medical assistance.**

---

## Diagnostic Support

Accurate diagnosis plays an important role in effective healthcare.

Diagnostic investigations may assist healthcare professionals in:

* Understanding a patient's condition
* Identifying potential health concerns
* Supporting treatment decisions
* Monitoring patient progress
* Supporting follow-up care

The exact laboratory and imaging services available at the hospital should be confirmed with hospital administration.

---

## In-Patient Care

Some medical conditions require patients to remain in the hospital for observation, treatment or recovery.

In-patient care may involve:

* Medical supervision
* Nursing support
* Medication administration
* Patient monitoring
* Treatment support
* Recovery and follow-up planning

Specific facilities and bed categories should be confirmed with the hospital.

---

## Preventive Healthcare

Preventive healthcare focuses on identifying potential health risks early and encouraging healthier lifestyles.

Depending on the patient's requirements, healthcare professionals may provide guidance related to:

* Routine health evaluation
* Lifestyle management
* Nutrition
* Health awareness
* Early identification of health concerns
* Follow-up monitoring

---

## Follow-Up & Continuity of Care

Healthcare does not end after a consultation or treatment.

Follow-up appointments can help healthcare professionals monitor recovery, review treatment progress and determine whether additional evaluation or care is required.

Patients are encouraged to retain their prescriptions, medical reports and previous investigation results for follow-up consultations.


4. Patient Information

# Patient Information

## Before Your Visit

To make your hospital visit easier, patients are encouraged to carry relevant healthcare documents and identification.

### Please Consider Bringing

* Government-issued identification
* Previous medical reports
* Previous prescriptions
* Laboratory reports
* Scan and imaging reports
* Current medication information
* Insurance documents, where applicable
* Referral documents, where applicable

---

## During Your Consultation

Please provide your healthcare professional with accurate information about:

* Your current symptoms
* Previous medical conditions
* Current medications
* Allergies
* Previous treatments
* Relevant medical reports

Accurate information can help healthcare professionals better understand your healthcare requirements.

---

## For Follow-Up Visits

Patients returning for follow-up should bring previous prescriptions, reports and discharge documents whenever applicable.

---

## For Hospital Admission

If hospitalisation is recommended, the hospital team will provide information regarding the admission process, required documentation and other relevant requirements.

Patients and attendants should follow hospital instructions regarding visiting hours, identification and other hospital policies.

---

## Insurance & Billing

Patients should contact the hospital administration to confirm:

* Accepted insurance providers
* Cashless treatment availability
* Required insurance documents
* TPA procedures
* Billing and payment methods
* Pre-authorisation requirements

Insurance coverage and approval are subject to the patient's insurance policy and insurer/TPA processes.
